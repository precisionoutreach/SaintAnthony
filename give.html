Where your gift goes

Worship and the sacraments · clergy and staff · our building and grounds · Church School and youth · hospitality and coffee hour · care for those in need. The parish council stewards every dollar, and giving statements are provided for tax purposes.

A word on the tithe

Orthodox Christians have always given the first portion — not the leftovers — back to God. Whether you begin with a tithe (10%) or a first step toward it, what matters is that it’s first, proportional, and joyful.

“Honor the Lord with your substance and with the first fruits of all your produce.”
— Proverbs 3:9
