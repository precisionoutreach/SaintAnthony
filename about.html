About our parish

Forty years of faith in north Houston.

We are an Orthodox Christian parish of the Antiochian Archdiocese — the Church of the ancient See of Antioch, “where the disciples were first called Christians” (Acts 11:26) — planted in Spring, Texas in 1982.

Our story

A parish built by parishioners

  • 1982
    A handful of families from St. George, our mother parish in Houston, begin an outreach with a vision: an Orthodox church for the growing communities of north Houston.
  • June 25, 1983
    The first Divine Liturgy is celebrated — the beginning of an unbroken rhythm of worship that continues every week to this day.
  • February 1, 1984
    The community is placed under the patronage of St. Anthony the Great, the father of monastics.
  • Today
    A full parish family on FM 2920 — worshiping, baptizing, teaching, serving Spring, Klein, Tomball, and The Woodlands, and welcoming inquirers from every background.

The Antiochian Archdiocese

We belong to the Antiochian Orthodox Christian Archdiocese of North America, Diocese of Wichita and Mid-America — in full communion with Orthodox Christians worldwide, in a Church whose faith and worship reach back to the apostles.

“Whoever you may be, always have God before your eyes; whatever you do, do it according to the testimony of the holy Scriptures.”

Our patron

St. Anthony the Great (c. 251–356)

Anthony was a young Egyptian who heard the Gospel read in church — “sell what you possess and give to the poor, and come, follow me” — and simply did it. He went into the desert to seek God in prayer, and the desert filled up behind him: thousands followed, and Christian monasticism was born.

He is called “the Great” not for armies or empires, but for humility, prayer, and a long life poured out for God and neighbor. His biography by St. Athanasius carried his witness across the ancient world.

We keep his feast on January 17 — our patronal feast, and one of the great celebrations of our parish year.

What we believe

The faith of the apostles

Orthodox Christianity is the original, historic Christian Church — unchanged in faith since the apostles, worshiping the Holy Trinity, confessing the Nicene Creed, and living the sacramental life Christ gave His Church. We are not a denomination that started somewhere along the way; we are the Church from which the story begins.

Curious? Don’t start with a book — start with a Sunday. Then, if you want to dig, we’ll put good books in your hands and a chair in our inquirers’ class.
