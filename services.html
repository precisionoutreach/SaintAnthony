Feasts & seasons ahead

Computed for the current year — Pascha and the movable cycle follow the Orthodox reckoning; fixed feasts follow the New Calendar used by our Archdiocese. Service times for each feast are announced in the bulletin.

    Keeping the fasts

    The four fasting seasons

    Great Lent & Holy Week

    The Fast of Forty Days before Pascha — the spring of the soul. Presanctified Liturgies, deep quiet, and the journey to the Cross and the empty tomb.

    Nativity Fast

    November 15 – December 24. Forty days of preparation for the coming of Christ in the flesh.

    Apostles’ Fast

    From the Monday after All Saints until the feast of Sts. Peter & Paul (June 29). Its length changes with the date of Pascha.

    Dormition Fast

    August 1–14. Two tender weeks with the Mother of God, with Paraklesis services, before her falling-asleep on August 15.

    Most Wednesdays and Fridays are also fast days. New to fasting? Don’t guess — talk with Fr. Anthony about a sustainable beginning.
