Your first Sunday

What it’s like, step by step

9:45 AM — Arrive

Park free on site at 7202 FM 2920. Come in the main doors — a greeter will welcome you, hand you a service book, and help you find a seat. Orthros (morning prayers) will already be underway; slipping in quietly is completely normal.

10:00 AM — Divine Liturgy

The Liturgy is sung almost entirely — ancient words, candlelight, and incense. Stand or sit as you’re comfortable; follow along in the book or simply take it in. It lasts about an hour and a half.

11:45 AM — Coffee hour

Everyone stays for food and conversation in the parish hall. This is the easiest place to ask questions — and Fr. Anthony would be glad to meet you. You will not leave hungry.

Good to know

A few honest notes

It will feel different — that’s okay. Orthodox worship is older than pews and projectors: chanted psalms, icons, incense, processions. Nobody expects you to know what to do.

Communion is for prepared Orthodox Christians, but at the end of the Liturgy everyone is welcome to come forward for a piece of the blessed bread (antidoron) — a sign of fellowship we’re glad to share with you.

Kids are fully part of the service. Wiggles, whispers, and the occasional squawk are the sound of a living parish. Children receive communion from infancy in the Orthodox Church.

Dress is “Sunday respectful” — most men wear collared shirts or better, most women dresses or the equivalent. Come as you are able; God looks at the heart.

FAQ

Common questions

How long is the service?
Divine Liturgy is about 90 minutes. With Orthros before it, the full Sunday morning runs from 9:00 to roughly 11:30, followed by coffee hour. Come for whatever portion you can.
Will I have to stand the whole time?
Standing is the traditional posture of Orthodox prayer, but seating is available and used freely. Stand when the congregation stands if you're able; sit whenever you need. Nobody is watching.
Is the service in English?
Yes — our parish worships predominantly in English, with occasional Arabic, Greek, or other languages reflecting the breadth of our parish family and our Antiochian heritage.
I’m not Orthodox. Am I really welcome?
Truly, yes. Most of our parish found Orthodoxy as adults — from every background imaginable. Come as many Sundays as you like with no expectations. If you ever want to go deeper, ask about our inquirers’ class.
What about my kids during the service?
Children stay in the Liturgy with their families — the Church is theirs too. Church School meets after communion on Sundays during the school year.
Where exactly are you?
7202 FM 2920, Spring, TX 77379 — on FM 2920 about a mile east of the Grand Parkway (99) and six miles west of I-45, serving Spring, Klein, Tomball, and The Woodlands. Free parking on site.
